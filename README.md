# CSE 4311 midterm prep

Personal study pages for the CSE 4311 / 5368 (Neural Networks) midterm.

- **Plan** (`index.html`): what is on the exam, a checklist for the night before and the morning of, and a one-screen last-look sheet.
- **Learn CNN** (`cnn.html`): convolutional neural networks from zero, with interactive convolution and pooling demos and a quiz.
- **Review & practice** (`review.html`): worked computational-graph, weight-shape and softmax/cross-entropy problems, revision sheets for every topic, and an MCQ drill.
- **Mock exam** (`mock.html`): 12 MCQs and 3 problems in the exam format.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Math is rendered with KaTeX from a CDN. Open `index.html` directly or serve the folder with `python3 -m http.server`.

These are my own notes and practice questions written while studying. The course's slides, notebooks and quizzes are not included.
